Why Japan

I spent 8 years of my life living in Japan, right through most of my formative years. It's where I learned to speak Japanese fluently and where I picked up a deep understanding of the culture — from the everyday customs to the way people do business. It never really stopped feeling like home.

Going Back

Long term, I want to move back overseas and build a career working with Asian companies, using both my MIS background and my Japanese to bridge the gap between American and Japanese businesses. Japan is the place I keep coming back to when I think about where I actually want to end up.
